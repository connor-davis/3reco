# Transactions

A transaction is the record of a completed trade: who sold to whom, which materials, what weight and what price, plus an invoice and optional receipts.

## Types

| Type | Meaning | How it is made |
|---|---|---|
| `c2b` | Collector to business | A business records a collection from one of its collectors |
| `b2b` | Business to business | A request is accepted, or a direct sale is recorded |

## Making one

```typescript
// A business records a collection
await fetch('/api/transactions/collections', {
  method: 'POST',
  credentials: 'include',
  headers: { 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    collector_id, business_id,
    items: [{ material_id, weight: '125', price: '6.50' }],
    collection_day: '2026-11-05',
  }),
})

// A direct business-to-business sale
await fetch('/api/transactions/sales', { /* same shape: { business_id, items } */ })
```

Weights and prices are decimal strings, never JSON numbers. A trade has up to 100 lines and no repeated material. Stock is checked and moved in the same database transaction, so a trade either happens completely or not at all.

## Invoice and receipts

- A PDF invoice is made for every trade and downloaded from `GET /api/transactions/:id/invoice`. See [Invoices](/docs/features/invoices).
- When you record a collection in the app you can add up to five receipt images in the same dialog. They upload right after the collection is saved. If one fails to upload, the collection is still recorded and you can add the receipt later from Invoice and receipts.
- The buyer, a business seller, admins and staff can attach up to five receipt images once the trade is complete (PNG or JPEG, 5 MiB each) with `POST /api/transactions/:id/receipts`. Staff can remove only receipts they added; admins can remove any.

## Seeing them

`GET /api/transactions/` lists your trades, `/purchases` the ones where you bought and `/sales` the business-to-business ones where you sold. Admins and staff can read every trade and see everything on their dashboard and exports. Other users can read only trades they took part in.

The total value of a trade is the sum of `weight × price` over its lines, worked out exactly on the server. See [Transactions API](/docs/api/transactions) for the full shape.
